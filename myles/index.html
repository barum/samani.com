7th Grade STEM Day

Five 45-minute blocks run between 9:00 and 2:00, with a 15-minute break between classes and a 30-minute lunch. Every block follows the same 5 / 20 / 10 / 10 split. After breaks and lunch the window holds exactly five classes, so Chemistry and Biology share block 4. Every other subject meets daily.

Block 4 science rotation

Day plan

Algebra 1, Geometry and Physics run before lunch. Physics comes after Algebra the same morning, so the algebra is fresh when Physics applies it.

    Inside every block

    All five blocks use the same four segments. Offsets are minutes into the block.

    1. :00–:055 min

      Review: last session

      Three questions on the last session's material, answered with notes closed, then checked together. Pulling material back from memory makes it last longer than rereading it does.

      The last session is usually yesterday. On Mondays it is Friday, and Chemistry and Biology can reach further back. Each review row in the day plan says how far.

    2. :05–:2520 min

      New material

      One concept. Work an example, then the student works a near-identical one while you watch.

      Stop at :25 even mid-topic. Whatever is left opens the next session's new-material segment.

    3. :25–:3510 min

      Review: today

      The student explains today's concept back without notes, then solves two check problems.

      Mistakes here set the questions for the next session's opening review.

    4. :35–:4510 min

      Practice

      Independent work with no help. Mix in one or two problems from earlier units so older material keeps getting used.

      In English, practice is writing: a paragraph or a set of sentences that uses today's skill.

    Flags

    Where this schedule departs from the request, and what is most likely to go wrong in practice.

    • Resolved

      Six 45-minute classes don't fit between 9:00 and 2:00

      WhyThe window is 300 minutes. Three 15-minute breaks take 45 and lunch takes 30, which leaves 225 minutes, enough for exactly five classes. Six classes would need 270.

      ResolutionLunch is 30 minutes so the 15-minute breaks fit; a one-hour lunch would push the end to 2:30. Algebra 1, Geometry, Physics and English meet every day, and Chemistry and Biology share block 4. If all six must meet daily, one more constraint has to give:

    • Spec change

      “Review of previous day” became “Review: last session”

      WhyChemistry and Biology don't meet every day, and nothing meets on weekends, so the previous school day's class is often a different subject. The opening review covers the last session of the same subject instead.

      EffectEach review row in the day plan says how far back it reaches: 1 day most of the time, 3 days on Mondays, and longer for the rotating sciences.

    • High

      Overruns eat practice or the break

      WhyEvery minute from 9:00 to 2:00 is assigned. When new material runs long, the overrun comes out of the 10-minute practice segment, which is the only work the student does alone, or out of the 15-minute break that follows.

      FixStop new material at :25 even mid-topic and carry the rest into the next session. The chime above sounds at every segment change.

    • High

      Geometry leans on Algebra 1 skills

      WhyGeometry courses are usually written for students who have finished Algebra 1, and this schedule runs both at once. Angle and segment problems need multi-step equations. Coordinate geometry needs slope and linear equations. The Pythagorean theorem needs square roots and radicals.

      FixStart Geometry with the units that need the least algebra, such as constructions, angle relationships and congruence. Hold each algebra-dependent unit until Algebra 1 has covered the skill it needs.

    • Medium

      Physics needs formula rearranging

      WhyQuantitative physics means solving a formula like d = v·t for whichever quantity is unknown. That is solving literal equations, an early Algebra 1 topic. Algebra runs before Physics every day, but the course still has to reach that unit.

      FixOpen Physics with conceptual and graphical work, such as motion graphs, force diagrams and energy transfers, until Algebra 1 covers literal equations.

    • Medium

      Biology can go 5 days between sessions

      WhyWith Chem-weighted rotation, Biology meets Tuesday and Thursday, so Tuesday's opening review reaches back 5 days to Thursday. That is the longest reach in the week.

      FixA/B weeks splits block 4 evenly over two weeks and cuts the longest gap to 4 days for both sciences. The cost is 18 Chemistry sessions a year (108 down to 90).

    • Medium

      Full labs don't fit inside a block

      WhyA lab needs setup, procedure and cleanup. The block gives it at most the 20-minute new-material segment.

      FixUse short demonstrations inside new material. Schedule full Chemistry and Biology labs after 2:00 PM.

    • Low

      Movement depends on how the breaks are used

      WhyThe three 15-minute breaks add up to 45 minutes. The CDC recommends 60 minutes or more of moderate-to-vigorous physical activity every day for ages 6 to 17.

      FixMake the breaks active and away from screens, then add at least 15 active minutes after 2:00 PM.
